# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Overview

This section presents key findings and business recommendations derived from the Business Performance, Ads Performance, and SEO & Sales Performance dashboards.

The analysis focuses on identifying business performance patterns, evaluating acquisition channel effectiveness, assessing content performance, and understanding sales team conversion effectiveness.

The findings are intended to support data-driven decision-making and identify potential areas for business improvement.

# 5. Sales Performance Insights

## 5.1 Chat Handling Performance by PIC

The PICs with the highest incoming and responded chat volumes were:

| Rank | PIC |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Sinta |
| 2 | Andi |
| 3 | Fajar |

**Business Insight:**

Sinta, Andi, and Fajar handled the highest volumes of incoming and responded discussions.

This indicates that chat handling volume is concentrated among these three PICs.

## 5.2 Chat-to-Order Conversion Performance

The conversion rate was calculated using responded chats as the denominator.

**Chat-to-Order Conversion Rate = Total Orders / Chat Response × 100%**

The conversion results were:

| Rank | PIC | Conversion Rate |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Rizky | 5.03% |
| 2 | Rendy | 4.87% |
| 3 | Nadia | 4.86% |
| 4 | Fajar | 2.63% |
| 5 | Sinta | 2.35% |
| 6 | Andi | 2.20% |

**Business Insight:**

Rizky recorded the highest chat-to-order conversion rate at 5.03%, followed by Rendy at 4.87% and Nadia at 4.86%.

Interestingly, the PICs handling the highest chat volumes—Sinta, Andi, and Fajar—recorded the lowest conversion rates among the six PICs.

This highlights a difference between operational workload and conversion effectiveness.

High chat volume does not necessarily translate into a higher conversion rate.

# 6. Business Recommendations

Based on the findings across the three dashboards, the following recommendations are proposed.

## 6.1 Improve Advertising Content Allocation

**Finding:**

Content 3 generated the highest results, while Content 6 achieved the lowest Cost per Result.

**Recommendations:**

- Maintain Content 3 as a benchmark for result generation.
- Evaluate opportunities to gradually scale Content 6 while monitoring its cost efficiency.
- Continue monitoring Content 1 because it performs well in both result volume and cost efficiency.
- Compare advertising costs and generated results regularly before reallocating the advertising budget.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve advertising expenditure efficiency while maintaining the volume of generated results.

## 6.2 Develop Region-Based Advertising Strategies

**Finding:**

Advertising content performance differs across geographical areas.

**Recommendations:**

- Evaluate content preferences and results for each geographical area.
- Develop region-specific advertising content based on observed performance.
- Monitor regional advertising expenditure and generated results.
- Standardize geographical data at a consistent administrative level before making direct regional comparisons.

**Expected Business Value:**

Support more targeted advertising strategies and improve the allocation of marketing resources across regions.

## 6.3 Optimize SEO Content Strategy

**Finding:**

Different articles lead in impressions, views, likes, and clicks.

**Recommendations:**

- Use high-impression articles as references for improving content exposure.
- Study high-view articles to understand which topics attract audience attention.
- Evaluate highly liked articles to identify topics that encourage audience engagement.
- Use high-click articles as references for content that attracts audience action.
- Review engagement performance across the content journey rather than relying on a single metric.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve organic content performance by identifying effective topics and understanding how audience engagement differs across articles.

## 6.4 Improve Sales Conversion Effectiveness

**Finding:**

PICs handling the highest chat volumes do not necessarily achieve the highest conversion rates.

**Recommendations:**

- Evaluate the customer-handling approaches used by high-converting PICs, particularly Rizky, Rendy, and Nadia.
- Identify opportunities to share effective communication and follow-up practices across the sales team.
- Review workload distribution and incoming lead characteristics across PICs.
- Monitor chat response and conversion performance together rather than evaluating sales productivity using chat volume alone.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve sales conversion effectiveness while maintaining balanced customer engagement and workload distribution.

## 6.5 Strengthen Application Process Monitoring

**Finding:**

A total of 230 applications remain within INP1–INP3, while 50 applications are waiting for stock availability and 66 are in the delivery process.

**Recommendations:**

- Monitor applications that have not completed the survey process.
- Review follow-up activities for applications in INP1–INP3.
- Track delivery progress for INPG applications.
- Monitor stock availability for INDT applications.
- Evaluate application aging to identify potential operational bottlenecks.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve visibility across the application lifecycle and support more effective operational follow-up.

## 6.6 Maintain Focus on Application Quality

**Finding:**

Best and Good SLIK classifications represent approximately 69.93% of reported applications and have a 100% ACC rate under the applicable business criteria.

**Recommendations:**

- Continue monitoring application quality by acquisition channel.
- Evaluate the contribution of Ads and Organic/SEO sources to favorable SLIK classifications.
- Maintain monitoring of ACC and TACC outcomes across SLIK categories.
- Use application quality indicators alongside application volume when evaluating acquisition effectiveness.

**Expected Business Value:**

Support acquisition strategies that consider both the quantity and quality of generated applications.

# 7. Overall Conclusion

The Business Performance Analytics project provides an integrated perspective on business performance across advertising, organic content, customer engagement, application outcomes, and sales productivity.

The analysis highlights several important findings:

1. Best and Good SLIK classifications account for the largest share of the reported application distribution.
2. Advertising content performance differs between result volume and cost efficiency.
3. Advertising content performance varies across geographical areas.
4. SEO articles demonstrate different strengths in impressions, views, likes, and clicks.
5. High chat handling volume does not necessarily correspond to high conversion effectiveness.
6. Application process monitoring provides opportunities to identify operational areas requiring follow-up.

Overall, the analysis demonstrates how SQL-based data preparation and analysis, Power BI data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboards can be combined to support business performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
