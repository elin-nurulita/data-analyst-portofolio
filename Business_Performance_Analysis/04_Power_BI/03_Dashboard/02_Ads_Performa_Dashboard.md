# Ads Performance Dashboard

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The Ads Performance Dashboard evaluates the effectiveness and efficiency of paid advertising activities.

It provides insights into advertising expenditure, generated results, audience engagement, individual content performance, and geographical distribution of advertising-generated results.

The dashboard helps identify high-performing advertising content, evaluate cost efficiency, and understand how advertising performance varies across different geographical areas.

## 2. Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Monitor total advertising expenditure and generated results.
- Evaluate advertising cost efficiency using Cost per Result.
- Compare advertising performance across individual content.
- Analyze audience exposure and engagement through impressions, reach, and link clicks.
- Identify geographical areas with higher advertising-generated results.
- Evaluate content performance across different geographical areas.

## 6. Key Business Findings

### 6.1 Top Advertising Content by Results

The three contents with the highest number of generated results were:

| Rank | Content |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Content 3 |
| 2 | Content 1 |
| 3 | Content 6 |

Content 3 generated the highest number of advertising results, followed by Content 1 and Content 6.

### 6.2 Top Advertising Content by Cost Efficiency

The three contents with the lowest Cost per Result were:

| Rank | Content |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Content 6 |
| 2 | Content 3 |
| 3 | Content 1 |

Content 6 achieved the lowest Cost per Result, indicating the highest cost efficiency among the evaluated contents.

### 6.3 Comparison of Results and Cost Efficiency

The comparison reveals different strengths among the leading advertising contents:

- Content 3 ranked first in generated results.
- Content 6 ranked first in cost efficiency.
- Content 1 appeared among the top three in both measurements.

This finding highlights that advertising content with the highest result volume is not necessarily the most cost-efficient.

Therefore, both result volume and cost efficiency should be considered when evaluating advertising performance.

### 6.4 Geographic Performance Insights

Advertising-generated results were concentrated in several reported geographical areas, with Banten ranking first, followed by Medan, Jawa Tengah, Jawa Barat, and Jakarta.

The leading contents also varied across geographical areas.

For example:
- Content 3 appeared among the leading contents in Jawa Barat and Banten.
- Content 8 appeared among the leading contents in Medan, Jawa Tengah, and Jakarta.
- Content 1 appeared among the leading contents in Jawa Tengah and Jawa Barat.

This indicates that advertising content performance varies across geographical areas and provides an opportunity to evaluate regional content preferences.
