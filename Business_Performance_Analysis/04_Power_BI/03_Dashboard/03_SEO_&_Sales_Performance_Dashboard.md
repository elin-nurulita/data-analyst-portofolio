# SEO & Sales Performance Dashboard

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The SEO & Sales Performance Dashboard evaluates organic content performance and sales team effectiveness.

The dashboard consists of two main analytical sections:

1. **SEO Performance:** Evaluates organic article performance based on generated results, impressions, views, likes, and clicks.
2. **Sales Performance:** Evaluates incoming chats, responded chats, and chat-to-order conversion rates across individual sales PICs.

The dashboard provides insights into organic marketing effectiveness and sales performance in handling customer interactions and generating applications.

## 2. Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Monitor the number of incoming messages and generated applications from organic activities.
- Evaluate SEO article performance based on audience exposure and engagement.
- Identify high-performing article groups and individual articles.
- Compare incoming and responded chats handled by each sales PIC.
- Evaluate individual PIC performance based on chat-to-order conversion rates.
- Identify opportunities to improve organic content effectiveness and sales conversion performance.

**Key Finding:**

Sinta handled the highest volume of incoming and responded chats, followed by Andi and Fajar.

These three PICs recorded the highest chat handling volumes among the sales team based on the reported ranking.

### 5.3 Chat Response-to-Order Conversion Rate by PIC

**Visualization:** Line Chart

**Analytical Purpose:**

To evaluate the effectiveness of each PIC in converting responded customer discussions into applications.

**Conversion Rate Formula:**

Chat-to-Order Conversion Rate = Total Orders / Chat Response × 100%

The calculation uses responded discussions as the denominator rather than total incoming discussions.

**Conversion Rate Results:**

| Rank | PIC | Conversion Rate |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Rizky | 5.03% |
| 2 | Rendy | 4.87% |
| 3 | Nadia | 4.86% |
| 4 | Fajar | 2.63% |
| 5 | Sinta | 2.35% |
| 6 | Andi | 2.20% |

**Key Findings:**

- Rizky achieved the highest chat-to-order conversion rate at 5.03%.
- Rendy and Nadia followed with 4.87% and 4.86%, respectively.
- Andi recorded the lowest conversion rate at 2.20%.
- Sinta, Andi, and Fajar handled the highest chat volumes but recorded the three lowest conversion rates.

### 5.4 Sales Performance Insights

The analysis reveals a difference between chat handling volume and conversion effectiveness.

The PICs with the highest chat volumes were not necessarily the PICs with the highest conversion rates.

This finding highlights the importance of evaluating sales performance using both operational workload and conversion effectiveness.

A high number of handled discussions does not necessarily translate into a higher proportion of generated applications.

## 6. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations are proposed.

### 6.1 Improve SEO Content Strategy

**Finding:**

Different articles perform best across impressions, views, likes, and clicks.

**Recommendations:**

- Use high-impression articles as references for improving organic content exposure.
- Study high-view articles to identify topics that attract audience attention.
- Evaluate highly liked articles to understand audience engagement.
- Use high-click articles as references for content that encourages audience action.
- Continue monitoring generated results by article group to identify topics that contribute to incoming customer discussions.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve organic content effectiveness by identifying topics that attract audiences and generate customer engagement.

### 6.2 Improve Sales Conversion Effectiveness

**Finding:**

Sinta, Andi, and Fajar handled the highest chat volumes, while Rizky, Rendy, and Nadia achieved the highest conversion rates.

**Recommendations:**

- Evaluate the customer-handling and follow-up approaches used by high-converting PICs.
- Identify effective communication practices that can be shared across the sales team.
- Review the differences in customer inquiries and lead characteristics handled by individual PICs.
- Monitor chat handling volume and conversion rates together.
- Evaluate opportunities to improve conversion performance among PICs with high chat volumes but lower conversion rates.

**Expected Business Value:**

Improve sales conversion effectiveness while maintaining efficient customer engagement and workload distribution.

### 6.3 Monitor Sales Performance Using Multiple Metrics

**Finding:**

Chat handling volume and conversion effectiveness represent different dimensions of sales performance.

**Recommendations:**

Evaluate individual PIC performance through multiple indicators, including:

- Incoming chat volume.
- Responded chat volume.
- Chat response-to-order conversion rate.
- Generated applications.

**Expected Business Value:**

Provide a more comprehensive and balanced evaluation of individual sales performance.
