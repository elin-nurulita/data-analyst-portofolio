# Business Performance Dashboard

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The Business Performance Dashboard provides an overall overview of business performance by monitoring advertising expenditure, customer engagement, application distribution, and sales outcomes.

The dashboard integrates information from advertising activities, customer conversations, and application records to help management evaluate business performance and identify potential areas for improvement.

## 2. Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Monitor overall advertising expenditure and customer engagement.
- Evaluate incoming messages and responded chats.
- Monitor application volume and order processing status.
- Analyze application distribution based on SLIK classifications.
- Compare paid advertising and organic acquisition channels based on application quality.
- Identify motorcycle series with the highest application volume.

## 4. Data Visualizations

### 4.1 SLIK Distribution

**Visualization:** Donut / Pie Chart

This visualization presents the distribution of applications across different SLIK classifications.

| SLIK Classification | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Best | 38.83% |
| Good | 31.10% |
| New | 18.99% |
| Bad | 11.17% |

**Analytical Purpose:**

To understand the distribution of application quality based on SLIK classifications.

**Key Finding:**

Best and Good classifications account for approximately 69.93% of the reported application distribution.

Under the applicable business criteria, applications classified as Best and Good have a 100% ACC rate.

### 4.2 Ads vs Organic

**Visualization:** Donut / Pie Chart

This visualization compares paid advertising and organic acquisition channels based on applications classified as Best and Good.

| Acquisition Channel | Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Ads | 63% |
| Organic / SEO | 36% |

**Analytical Purpose:**

To identify which acquisition channel generates a higher number of applications with favorable SLIK classifications.

**Key Finding:**

Paid advertising contributes a larger share of applications with Best and Good SLIK classifications compared with organic/SEO channels.

### 4.3 Order Status Distribution

This visualization displays application distribution based on different order processing statuses.

| Order Status | Business Description | Applications |
|---|---|---:|
| CLSD | Delivered orders | 68 |
| INPG | In delivery process | 66 |
| INDT | Waiting for stock availability | 50 |
| INP1–INP3 | Survey process not yet completed | 230 |
| INP4–INP5 | Survey completed, with ACC/TACC outcomes | 302 |
| **Total** | | **716** |

**Analytical Purpose:**

To monitor application progress across different processing stages and identify potential operational bottlenecks.

**Key Finding:**

The largest group consists of applications in INP4–INP5, with 302 applications, followed by INP1–INP3 with 230 applications.

This indicates that a substantial number of applications have reached the survey outcome stage, while others are still awaiting survey completion.

## 5. Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important findings:

1. Best and Good SLIK classifications account for approximately 69.93% of the reported application distribution.
2. Ads contribute a larger share of applications with favorable SLIK classifications compared with Organic/SEO channels.
3. INP4–INP5 represents the largest application processing group, with 302 applications.
4. A total of 230 applications remain in the INP1–INP3 survey processing stages.
5. Honda ADV 160 ranks first in application volume among the four motorcycle series.

## 6. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

### 6.1 Monitor Application Quality

Continue monitoring application quality based on SLIK classifications and evaluate the contribution of each acquisition channel to favorable application outcomes.

### 6.2 Improve Application Follow-up

Monitor applications within INP1–INP3 to identify pending survey processes and improve follow-up activities.

### 6.3 Monitor Delivery and Stock Availability

Track applications in INPG and INDT to improve visibility into delivery progress and stock availability.

### 6.4 Evaluate Product Demand

Use motorcycle series performance to support product-level marketing evaluation and sales planning.

## 7. Analytical Questions

This dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the total advertising expenditure?
2. How many incoming messages have been received and responded to?
3. How many applications have been generated, delivered, or categorized as indent?
4. What is the distribution of applications across SLIK classifications?
5. Which acquisition channel generates more applications with Best and Good SLIK classifications?
6. How are applications distributed across different order statuses?
7. Which motorcycle series generate the most applications?

## 8. Conclusion

The Business Performance Dashboard provides a consolidated overview of advertising expenditure, customer engagement, application quality, and order processing performance.

By combining marketing and application information, the dashboard enables management to monitor business performance, evaluate acquisition channel contributions, and identify operational areas that require further attention.
