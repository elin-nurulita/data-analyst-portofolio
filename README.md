## Projects

### 1. [Motorcycle Business Performance Analytics](./Business_Performance_Analysis/)

**Tools:** Google BigQuery (SQL), Power BI, DAX, Microsoft Excel.

**Overview:**

An end-to-end business analytics project developed to evaluate business performance across paid advertising, organic content, customer engagement, and sales activities within the motorcycle business sector.

The project integrates four business datasets — Ads, Artikel, Chat, and Pesanan — to provide insights into marketing effectiveness, application outcomes, SEO content performance, and sales productivity.

**Key Highlights:**
- Processed and analyzed four business datasets using SQL in Google BigQuery.
- Performed data cleaning, type standardization, data transformation, and duplicate checks.
- Conducted Exploratory Data Analysis and business performance analysis using SQL.
- Integrated four datasets through Power BI data modeling.
- Developed 10 DAX measures for business performance monitoring and KPI calculations.
- Built three dashboard pages covering Business Performance, Ads Performance, and SEO & Sales Performance.

**Key Findings:**
- Best and Good SLIK classifications represented approximately 69.93% of the reported application distribution.
- Content 3 generated the highest advertising results, while Content 6 achieved the lowest cost per result.
- SEO articles demonstrated different strengths across impressions, views, likes, and clicks.
- Rizky achieved the highest chat-to-order conversion rate at 5.03%, while high chat volume did not necessarily correspond to higher conversion effectiveness.

**Business Value:**

The analysis provides an integrated view of marketing effectiveness, customer engagement, application quality, and sales productivity to support data-driven business evaluation and decision-making.

### 4. [Macroeconomic Data Analysis: Inflation, Interest Rate, and Unemployment (2000–2017)](./macroeconomic-analysis/)

**Tools:** Google BigQuery.

**Overview:**

A cross-country analysis covering 266 countries and 17 years of historical data to explore the interaction between inflation, unemployment, and interest rates.

This study evaluates whether real-world data align with classical macroeconomic theories.

**Key Findings:**
- Inflation and interest rates move in opposite directions, differing from Fisher's theory.
- Interest rates and unemployment move together, showing tightening policy effects.
- Differences across countries are influenced by structural and policy contexts.
